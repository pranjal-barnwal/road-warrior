# Presentation Evaluation Rubric

This file is a cleaned version of the scoring criteria used to evaluate the final kata presentation.

## 1. Functional and Non-Functional Requirements

**Question**
How well did the team define the functional and non-functional requirements?

**What reviewers are looking for**

- Clear system capabilities
- Explicit non-functional requirements such as performance, security, scalability, availability, and maintainability

**Scoring guide**

- Incomplete or unclear requirements
- Mostly clear, with some gaps
- Clear, complete, and well-structured

## 2. Logical Architecture

**Question**
How clear and understandable was the logical architecture?

**What reviewers are looking for**

- Clear components, services, and data stores
- Clear interactions between major parts of the system
- Simple, readable architecture communication

**Scoring guide**

- Hard to follow or missing key components
- Understandable but could be clearer
- Clear, accurate, and easy to understand

## 3. Physical Architecture

**Question**
How well did the team present the physical deployment architecture?

**What reviewers are looking for**

- Clear explanation of what runs where
- Realistic deployment view including infrastructure, containers or nodes, cloud services, and networking assumptions
- Clear understanding of how the system is deployed in practice

**Scoring guide**

- Hard to follow or missing key components
- Understandable but could be clearer
- Clear, accurate, and easy to understand

## 4. Solution Strategy: Mapping Requirements to Decisions

**Question**
Did the team explain how their architectural decisions satisfy the requirements?

**What reviewers are looking for**

- Clear reasoning behind each major decision
- Traceability from requirements and quality attributes to architectural choices
- Logical and defensible technology selection

**Scoring guide**

- Decisions not clearly linked to requirements
- Some mapping, but not fully clear
- Clear and logical mapping of decisions to requirements

## 5. ADR Summary (Optional)

**Question**
Did the team provide a short and clear list of key architectural decisions?

**What reviewers are looking for**

- Concise 1-2 line decisions
- Clear rationale that still fits presentation pacing

**Scoring guide**

- Missing or unclear ADRs
- Some ADRs listed but lacking clarity
- Clear, concise ADR list
